About The Artist

Crafting Human-Centered Digital Artistry

Hello! I'm Elena Vance, a multidisciplinary digital artist, lead UI/UX designer, and web accessibility advocate based in San Francisco. With over a decade of experience bridging the gap between artistic visual storytelling and technical engineering, I create digital experiences that delight the senses while ensuring everyone can interact effortlessly.

My philosophy centers on Universal Design: beauty should never come at the expense of usability or accessibility. Every line of code I write, every color palette I curate, and every interface I prototype adheres strictly to the WCAG 2.1 AAA accessibility benchmarks, proving that high-end modern web aesthetics and full inclusion go hand-in-hand.

WCAG AAA Accessibility

Semantic HTML5, ARIA patterns, focus control & high contrast modes.

High Performance

Zero-dependency vanilla CSS/JS, lazy loading & optimized assets.

Spatial & 3D Systems

Generative art shaders, interactive visualizers & cross-platform design.
